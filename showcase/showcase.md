# Wick

The city is asleep. The lamp is lit. Everything else is coming.

You are the last lamplighter, walking an endless street toward a dawn ten minutes away. You only steer your footsteps; your tools strike, circle, spark, and burn on their own. Stay close enough to gather what falls, and far enough away to keep the flame alive.

Every gem feeds the lamp. Every level offers a choice: sharpen a familiar tool, learn a new one, or carry a trinket that changes the whole night. Six tools and six trinkets leave room for a plan, but never for everything. The great creatures leave chests; bring a fully honed tool and its matching trinket, and watch it become something brighter.

Moths give way to swarms, armored beetles, weaving wisps, and things that move too quickly through the dark. At nine minutes, the night itself starts following. Keep an escape route. Keep walking. Keep the light until dawn.
